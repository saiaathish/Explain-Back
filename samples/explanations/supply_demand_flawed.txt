Demand slopes upward because higher prices make consumers buy more, and supply slopes downward because firms offer less when price rises. Equilibrium is the highest price consumers will tolerate.

A price ceiling above equilibrium is always binding and creates a shortage because it makes buyers demand more than firms supply. It also shifts the demand curve to the left.

A tax legally placed on sellers is always paid only by sellers, so buyers pay no more and the quantity traded stays unchanged.
