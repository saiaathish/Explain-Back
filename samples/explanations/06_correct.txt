The pump exports three sodium ions and imports two potassium ions per cycle. This creates a small electrical imbalance because three positive charges leave while only two positive charges enter. The transport requires ATP because both ions move against their concentration gradients. ATP phosphorylates the pump, which changes its shape and exposes sodium to the outside. Potassium binding and phosphate release then return the pump to its original shape, so potassium is released inside the cell.
